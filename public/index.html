KNOW YOUR WASTE

Waste comes in many forms

Different materials need different handling.

🧴

Plastic

Check the resin code and local recycling rules.

Tip: Keep accepted containers appropriately clean.
📦

Paper & Cardboard

Keep recyclable paper dry and separate heavily contaminated packaging.

Tip: Flatten boxes when practical.
🍃

Organic

Food scraps may be compostable where suitable facilities exist.

Tip: Follow local composting guidance.
🔋

Electronic

Batteries and electronics need appropriate collection and handling.

Tip: Keep damaged batteries out of ordinary bins.

Recycling options depend on your location and available facilities.
